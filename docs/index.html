POOR MAN'S BEIER

flash your DIYGuy-999 ESP32 right from this browser - no downloads

Browser not supported

WebSerial required. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer (Windows / Mac / Linux / ChromeOS). Mobile browsers and Safari/Firefox don't support direct USB flashing yet.

1. Pick engine sound

2. Pick remote protocol

3. Plug in & flash

1
Plug the ESP32 into your computer with a USB cable. (CH340 or CP210x driver may be needed first time.)
2
Click Install below, then pick the COM/serial port that appears.
3
If it stalls, hold the BOOT button while clicking install.
